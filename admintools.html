Auto timetable

Builds the printable AutoTimetable sheet from the Timetable sheet (Room | Section | Period I…V, every section = Subject / Teacher / Days rows; a missing teacher is shaded). Tick the programs that belong to this printed timetable — the science programs are ticked as before; tick F.A for the Arts one.

Teacher workload by status

Reads Science Timetable, ARTS TIMETABLE and DropdownData and writes three sheets: Permanent Workload, Morning Visiting Workload, Evening Visiting Workload (department-wise, S.No · Teacher · Designation · Classes with days · WORKLOAD), then saves the print-ready WORKLOAD dd-MMM-yyyy.xlsx next to the workbook in Drive. Evening science sections: L2, M2, N2, O2, P2, S1, T1.

Stopping merit

From Portal backup: open quota, 1st Year only. Merit % = (obtained + 20 if Hafiz-e-Quran) ÷ original total × 100, also shown on the common 1200 scale. Writes the Stopping Merit sheet (program · category · sections · last admitted student).

Admission slips

4 slips on every A4 landscape page with dotted cut lines · Roll# = Enroll# · section, category (quota), session, student and father name, the 7 subjects and the student's signature line. Sorted by program, then Enroll#.

Fill room from timetable

Reads Program + Section + Room from the Timetable sheet and writes the room into every sheet of the destination workbook (matching ProgramName + Section; “F.A (JCW)” and “F.A (Jinnah College)” count as the same program). A section with two different rooms in the timetable keeps the first one.
